A graduate course in the numerical methods that underlie modern computation — from floating-point arithmetic to automatic differentiation — built for a world where AI tools sit on every desk.

How this course works

AI tools — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini — are required parts of every assignment. They're fast at producing answers. What they can't do for you is understand them.

The bar isn't a correct output — it's a clear explanation. Homework in this course uses AI tools deliberately, but grading centers on whether you can explain, to me and to your peers, what you learned and why the method works. Getting a working answer from a model is the easy part; being able to teach it back is the point.
